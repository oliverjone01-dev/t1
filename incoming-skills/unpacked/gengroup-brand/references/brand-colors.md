# GENGROUP Brand Colors — Detailed Reference

## GENGLASS (Flagship, Loft-Modern)
| Role | HEX | RGB | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary Dark | #0A0A0A | 10,10,10 | Backgrounds |
| Secondary Dark | #111111 | 17,17,17 | Cards, panels |
| Gold Accent | #C8A951 | 200,169,81 | CTAs, prices, highlights |
| White | #FFFFFF | 255,255,255 | Headlines, key text |
| Gray | #999999 | 153,153,153 | Body text, captions |
| Industrial | #333333 | 51,51,51 | Borders, subtle elements |
| Glass Tint | #E8F0F0 | 232,240,240 | Glass product overlays |

**Usage rules:** Gold maximum 15% of visual area. Dark backgrounds mandatory for digital. White backgrounds allowed for print catalogs only.
